Welcome to TaskGuard

Declare your intent. Stay on track.

1
Set your intent Click the TaskGuard icon, describe what you're working on, and pick a focus duration.
2
Build your allowlist Add sites you need for your work (e.g. docs.python.org, github.com) so they're never interrupted.
3
Stay accountable When you visit an unlisted site, TaskGuard will ask: "Is this relevant?" You decide — it learns from your choices.
Heads up: Once a session starts, all currently open tabs that aren't on your allowlist will be intercepted. Add sites you need to your allowlist first.
